Most-differentiated SNPs between arrangements, and their codon class
I = BB (inverted), S = AA. dp = |p_AA - p_BB|.

READ THE CAVEAT FIRST: recombination between arrangements is
suppressed across the whole block, so high dp is expected EVERYWHERE
inside the inversion. Individual high-dp SNPs are passengers, not
candidate causal variants. Only the 0-fold vs 4-fold COMPOSITION of
the differentiated tail is interpretable, and only against the
collinear control.
