Neutrality at the WEIGHTED best-fit history (inverted = BB, p_now = 0.374)
fitted history: arose ~850 ka, held near p_hist = 0.74, fell to 0.374 over ~100 ky ending ~175 ka
(supersedes neutrality_check.txt, which is the earlier unweighted p_hist = 0.7 epoch, NOTES sec 8.17)

==========================================================================
1. ATTAINMENT -- could drift take it to the frequency it reached?
==========================================================================
  The relevant target is the HIGHEST frequency inferred, p_hist, not
  the present one: a neutral allele has to get up there before it can
  come back down.

  Ne                              x=0.374 (now)    x=0.74 (p_hist)
  N_ANC = 547,928                       473,378          1,154,383
  N(t=850 ka)                           473,378          1,154,383
  N(t=275 ka)                         2,390,251          5,828,887
  N0 = 6,808,096                      5,881,796         14,343,402

  coefficients: E[t|reach x] = 0.864*N at x=0.374, 2.107*N at x=0.74
  (the old, wrong reading used x=0.626: 1.650*N; the unweighted refit used x=0.70: 1.936*N)

  Reaching p_hist = 0.74 by drift takes 1,154,383-14,343,402 generations,
  against a fitted total age of 850,000.
  BINDING case (smallest Ne, N_ANC): needs 1.36x the inversion's
  entire lifetime; widens to 17x at N0 as N grows 12.4x.
  (At the earlier unweighted p_hist = 0.7 the binding margin was 1.25x, so weighting the fit strengthened this.)

  P(a new neutral inversion ever reaching 0.74) = 1.23e-06 to 9.92e-08

    t_fall   drift SD  observed fall (SD)

  This bounds DRIFT only (diffusion for an unlinked neutral allele); it
