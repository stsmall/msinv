READING IT
============================================================================
  'AA anc' is the share of diagnostic sites where the AA majority
  allele equals the ancestral base. >50% means AA carries ancestral
  states more often, i.e. AA is the ANCESTRAL arrangement and BB is
  derived -- the OPPOSITE of the recorded polarization. <50% supports
  the recorded 'AA = derived'.
  In the collinear control both columns must sit at ~50%; if they do
  not, the estimator is biased and neither region can be read.
  Where the two methods disagree, trust PARSIMONY: est-sfs's ingroup
  frequency term pushes it toward calling the commoner arrangement's
  allele ancestral, and AA is the commoner arrangement (0.626).
