THE CONTROL IS THE WRONG YARDSTICK -- retest against the BLOCK
============================================================================
  pi_AA sits ~23% below pi_BB throughout the inversion. That is the
  project's central observation (pi_I/pi_S = 1.356 with I = BB), not a
  sweep -- so ANY test standardised on the collinear control flags AA
  across the whole block. The candidates above are that shift, not
  localised events. The right null is the body's OWN ratio
  distribution.

  pi_AA/pi_BB across 1994 body windows: median 0.787, sd 0.195
  windows > 2 SD below the body's own median: 3 of 1994 (0.2%) -- 2.3% expected if Normal.
  The block is LIGHT-tailed. There is no localised excess.
  Of the 44 control-standardised candidates, only 2 survive this test,
  and they spread over 17 distinct 0.5 Mb bins across 64.1-79.3 Mb -- not one focal region.

  VERDICT: no within-arrangement sweep signal in either class. Note
  the direction too -- ZERO BB-specific windows by any criterion, and
  BB carries HIGHER pi than AA throughout. The derived arrangement,
  which is where a supergene's adaptive variant would sit, shows no
  sweep signature at all.

  POWER CAVEAT: 50 kb windows, SFS statistics only (haplotype stats
  are degenerate on unphased data), BB n = 95. A weak or old sweep
  would be missed, and one predating the inversion would be shared by
  both classes and invisible to an asymmetry test.
